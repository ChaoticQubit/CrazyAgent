  Market research: Fieldnotes Mobile

  Ecologists keep field notes on paper or in general note apps that lose data
  without signal. Most want one offline notebook that syncs later.

  Who pays
  - University ecology labs, mostly grant funded
  - Park and reserve services
  - Environmental consultancies
